## 2. For customers (`/chat`, `/case/{id}`) *(pending UI)*
1. **Start:** choose a demo customer; an OTP appears on screen (mock identity — in a real bank it arrives by SMS or app).
2. **Describe the charge** in Spanish or Portuguese: amount, date, merchant if you know it.
3. **Confirm** the transaction if the agent shows options.
4. **Receipt:** what was done (verified), the case number, the legal deadline with its source, and what a person does
   next.
5. **Follow your case** at `/case/{id}`: timeline, countdown to the deadline, "request a call", "add information".
6. **Notifications:** link Telegram or confirm an e-mail address to receive each status change.

What the agent will never do: show another customer's data, report an action it could not verify, promise a refund the
policy did not grant, or ask for your card number, CVV or password.

## 3. For dispute analysts (`/login`, `/console`) *(pending UI)*
1. **Sign in** with your account (Cognito; first login asks for a new password).
2. **Inbox:** cases by status, zone color and SLA; MX debit cases rise in priority on business day 1.
3. **Open a case:** the handoff card shows verified facts with links to their evidence, the actions taken, open
   questions, the deadline and the copilot's proposal.
4. **Decide:** approve credit, approve or undo a block, request information, mark as ambiguous, resolve, close. Every
   click is an audited event with your user and reason; the customer is notified.
5. **Supervised mode:** when on, every action needs human approval (banner at the top).

## 4. Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Your session expired" | 15-minute session TTL | Request a new OTP; nothing is lost |
| "Action not confirmed" | A tool did not answer; the agent escalated | A person completes the action; check `/case/{id}` |
| Empty console | Demo data was reset | Use "Load demo cases" *(pending, spec 05)* |
| A page shows an error id | The error carries a `trace_id` | Share the id; it locates the run in the logs |
