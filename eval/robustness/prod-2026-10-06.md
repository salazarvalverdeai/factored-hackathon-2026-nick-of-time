## 4. Why G-IN-03 fires on benign turns

Reproduced locally, no production call: the scripted messages of the report and the load test through B0
(`nick_of_time.nlu`), `turn_language` (D-087) and the real graph `dispute_intake` on the real local MCP server
(`tests/local_mcp.py`, MemoryStore, fixture gold of the dev case), arms S0 and S1 on the `fake` provider (S1 on `fake`
has no price, so it runs as S0, D-058; on prod S1 adds a Haiku `understand` call below τ). The decision and its rule ids
come from the `decide` step's trace record (D-046).

**Root cause.** G-IN-03 is not raised by language detection on these turns. It is the guardrail that
`contracts/policies.yaml` attaches to `POL-CLARIFY` and `POL-CLARIFY-EXHAUSTED` (rules lines 13–14), and
`PolicyEngine._result` (`packages/nick_of_time/policy/engine.py`) reports the guardrail of every rule that fired. So
**every `ask` decision is reported as G-IN-03 "language and ambiguity"**, whatever the reason for asking. Three kinds of
benign turn reach `ask`:

1. **No charge named yet, the main source, and by design.** A clear dispute (intent ≥ τ = 0.80) that names no amount,
   date or merchant has 0 candidates, and rule 5 (`candidates != 1`) asks which charge. It is the first turn of the
   canonical demo flow ("no reconozco un cargo"), so G-IN-03 shows on nearly every conversation. The eval set encodes
   this meaning: two of the three dev `ambiguous` cases take exactly this path (EV-0109 and EV-0111: 0 candidates,
   `POL-CLARIFY`, G-IN-03; EV-0110: 3 candidates), and their `expected.guardrail_ids` require G-IN-03
   (`eval/harness/compare.py` fails a run that misses an expected guardrail). The D-085 "show my latest charges" list is
   the same rule. Working as specified; what misleads is the name ("language and ambiguity") and the console label
   (`apps/web/messages/trace.ts`: "Language or ambiguity: asked to clarify"), which read as a guard event.
2. **B0 recall gaps on clear disputes, a real defect.** B0's unrecognized-charge pattern (`nlu/rules.py`,
   `(?:no|nao) (?:reconozco|…|fui yo|fui eu|…)`) needs the verb right after the negation, so "no **lo** reconozco" and
   "yo no fui" are not matched. The message falls to `out_of_scope` at 0.5 and D-032 asks, even when the charge was found
   (terse 2: 1 candidate, still `ask`), and the customer is asked for details they already gave (code_switching 1 on
   prod). The next short reply ("si") then counts as the third clarification and exhausts the budget: handoff with a
   general call, G-IN-03 again. On prod, S1's `understand` runs below τ and may recover some of these; it did not for
   code_switching 1.
3. **Short acknowledgements** ("si", "Tá bom, obrigado") are `out_of_scope` at 0.5 and count as another clarification
   turn when no case is open.

Ruled out, with the evidence above: language detection (B0's language equals the session language on every row but
code_switching 1, and `other_language` is false on all of them, so the G-IN-03 "other language" route in
`intake.route` never fired); the D-087 `min_words=2` threshold (it only picks the reply language; "cargo raro", "si"
and "Tá bom, obrigado" fall back to the last or session language, which is correct, and it does not feed the policy);
tone (all-caps aggressive turn 1 reads `urgent` and is not an ask; tone only changes the template's opening line);
merchant names; and chips with no text (a pressed chip skips the classifier with confidence 1.0, AC-32).

**Proposed minimal fix (none made here; each needs its owner's PR).**
1. **Now, no behaviour change: say what G-IN-03 means.** Rename the guardrail to "language, ambiguity or missing
   details: ask" in `contracts/policies.yaml` (display text only; the lead's approval, constitution #9) and the console
   label in `apps/web/messages/trace.ts` to "Asked to clarify (which charge, ambiguity or language)". Readers of traces,
   reports and the console then stop reading slot filling as a guard event. Risk: none for decisions, eval or the
   auditor (ids unchanged); `apps/web/lib/trace.test.ts`, which pins the English label, changes with it.
2. **Next, the real defect: B0 recall.** Allow an object clitic between the negation and the verb
   (`(?:no|nao) (?:(?:lo|la|o|a) )?(?:reconozco|reconheco|…)`) and accept "yo no fui" / "eu não fui" as unrecognized.
   This removes the wrong asks of terse 2 and code_switching 1 and the clarification-exhausted handoff that follows.
   Risk: B0 is a benchmarked arm (spec 11, spec 15), so its dev numbers move and must be re-reported; the sealed
   protocol-v1 held-out results stay as recorded, and a re-run after the change is a new run, not compared silently.
   False-positive risk is low (a negated "recognize" next to a charge is a dispute).
3. **Not recommended before the sealed evaluation is closed:** splitting the rule (a `POL-ASK-DETAILS` with no
   guardrail for a clear dispute with 0 candidates). It is the cleaner semantics, but EV-0109 and EV-0111 and, by the
   same derivation (`eval/derive_expected.py`), held-out rows with 0 candidates expect G-IN-03, so it would turn passing
   runs into guardrail mismatches unless the expectations are re-derived.

## 5. Spend

The customer projection carries no usage (D-013), so a public client cannot measure spend; the exact figure is the sum
of `llm_calls.cost_usd` for this run's `demo-` run ids (console or database).

- Gate before sending `[projected]`, from `eval/bench/prices.yaml` (Haiku 4.5 1.10 / 5.50 and Sonnet 4.6 3.30 / 16.50
  USD per 1M input / output tokens `[external]`, read 2026-10-04): an upper bound of **0.021 USD per turn**, one S1
  `understand` call and one S2 writer call per turn, each with its full system prompt, a large payload and its
  `max_tokens` (`prod_loadtest.projected_usd`, `[assumption]` on the payload sizes). For the whole PRODRUN (15 turns on
  2026-10-05, 8 + 40 here): **≤ 1.32 USD**, under the 1.5 USD cap, so the run went ahead. The 48 turns of 2026-10-06 UTC are
  ≤ 1.01 USD of that day's shared 10 USD cap.
- Likely spend `[assumption]`: the replies carry the template wording verbatim ("Caso K-… abierto y verificado
  (verificación V-…)"), which the S2 writer would have rewritten, so the console's writer setting was `template` and
  only S1 `understand` was billed, and only on turns below τ. With the same bound for `understand` alone
  (≤ 0.0036 USD per turn) the whole PRODRUN is ≤ 0.23 USD, and at the robustness README's typical call size (about 290 input and
  80 output tokens, about 0.0008 USD) about 0.05 USD.
